    Watch your suit O2 outside — refill at the Habitat or Oxygen Station. Keep the base powered: solar electricity runs oxygen, water and thermal control. Every repair consumes Earth Cargo spare parts (press I) — they are finite and cannot be resupplied, so prioritize. Collect rocks for bypass scrap and soil / ice samples for the lab. Repairs are hands-on minigames — no magic buttons. Sleep at the Habitat to advance to the next day.

    Use Mission Control to set power priorities, run the greenhouse, and hold emergency reserves — every choice trades one resource for another. Watch the radiation chip: during a solar particle event, get to the Habitat shelter. Repairs follow Detect → Diagnose → Repair → Verify → Restore, and the NASA DATA panel shows the real measurements behind the simulation.

    Launch needs: ship readiness 100%, 3 lab analyses, 4 repairs, 10 materials collected, all systems green, and the Day-7 window. Emergency evacuation exists if things go badly wrong.
